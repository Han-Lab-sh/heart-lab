Research

In an era shaped by the global pursuit of carbon neutrality and the rapidly growing energy demands of artificial intelligence, the sustainable management of water, energy, and resources has emerged as a critical societal challenge. The HEART Lab addresses these interconnected challenges through electrochemical approaches.

Our team pursues a fundamental understanding of the physicochemical phenomena governing electrochemical systems across multiple length scales, from molecular-level interactions to device-scale processes. Our research encompasses electrocatalysis, interfacial phenomena, ion transport, and local pH dynamics, with particular emphasis on their complex interplay within electrochemical environments. By elucidating these coupled phenomena, we aim to bridge fundamental electrochemical science and practical engineering, establishing scientific foundations for the development of efficient, sustainable, and industrially relevant electrochemical technologies.

Sanghwi Han was born in Busan and graduated from Seoul National University (SNU) in 2019. He subsequently served for two years as a commissioned officer in the Republic of Korea Marine Corps before returning to SNU to pursue his Ph.D. under the supervision of Prof. Jeyong Yoon. Following his doctoral studies, he conducted postdoctoral research with Prof. Shannon W. Boettcher at the University of California, Berkeley, and Prof. Joaquin Resasco at the University of Texas at Austin.

His research focuses on elucidating the fundamental physicochemical principles governing electrochemical systems across multiple length scales, from molecular-level interactions to device-scale phenomena. Driven by the belief that fundamental science should inform practical innovation, he seeks to bridge the gap between scientific discovery and industrial application through the development of functional materials and electrochemical systems that address pressing energy and environmental challenges.
